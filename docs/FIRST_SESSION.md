# First session and closing the game

## Recommended starting point

For your first VR session, start at **Franklin and Lamar**, after the North Yankton prologue. If you already have a save at that point, copy your normal GTA saves into your Suite profile and load it in Story Mode. Keep a copy if you want to return to that starting point. [Profiles and saves](SAVES.md)

If the camera, your position, or your body pose is out of alignment, select **VR Menu → Recenter**. This usually resolves those alignment problems. [How to recenter](TROUBLESHOOTING.md#recenter-the-view)

## Starting a new game

Open your profile's **Save** tab and choose **Start a new story**. The **first switch to Trevor** in North Yankton can fail with motion controllers when the guard grabs Michael. The character wheel can close when you move the thumbstick to select Trevor.

1. When the mission asks you to switch, focus the GTA window and press **F10** to turn VR off.
2. With a keyboard and mouse, hold **Left Alt**, move the mouse toward **Trevor**, and release **Alt** to confirm. You can also switch with a standard gamepad.
3. Once you control Trevor, press **F10** again to return to VR.

If another part of the prologue does not advance in VR, finish that section or the entire prologue with VR off. When you reach **Franklin and Lamar**, switch VR back on and save when the game lets you so you can resume there later.

Once Story Mode unlocks multiple protagonists, open **VR Menu → Story Character** to choose one. [VR menu and Trainer](VR_MENU.md)

If a cutscene makes you feel motion sick, skip it when the game offers that option. If a transition leaves the camera out of place, select **VR Menu → Recenter** when you regain control. [Story Mode known issues](KNOWN_ISSUES.md#story-mode-camera-and-cutscenes)

## Close the game and play again

Save your progress, then select **Force GTA to close** in the Suite. Quitting from the pause menu can leave a GTA process running after the window closes.

If the Suite cannot stop the process, open Task Manager, locate the GTAVXR session process (`GTAVXR_SourceBaseline.exe`), and select **End task**. Wait for the Suite to finish closing the session before clicking **Play** again.

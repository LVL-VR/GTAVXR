# Profiles and saves

Each profile has its own GTAVXR version, mod selection, VR settings, and saves.

The first profile starts with a copy of your GTA V Legacy saves. You can continue your campaign in GTAVXR while keeping the original starting point in normal GTA.

Open the profile's **Save** tab to select **Copy saves from normal GTA** or **Start a new story**. When you replace a profile's saves, the Suite keeps the previous set in that profile's history.

When creating a profile, choose **Copy from normal GTA** or **New story**. You can keep one profile for your current campaign and another for a fresh start.

Save your progress in GTA. When the session closes, the Suite writes the session's saves back to the profile you launched.
